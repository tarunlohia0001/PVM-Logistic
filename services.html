LOGISTICS

PVM Logistics is committed to providing complete logistical solutions to its clients. From warehousing to last mile delivery, PVM works as a trusted partner for your supply-chain needs. PVM prides itself on its underlying principle of innovation, and we create tailor-made logistical solutions keeping in mind the product being moved.

WAREHOUSING & FULFILLMENT

Storage capabilities are a part of our key USP’s. Seven warehouses, both dry and cold, spread over 3 lakh sq. ft., allow our professionals to meet your diverse storage needs. As your logistics partner, PVM Logistics ensures that your products are always available to be shipped at the press of a button. Quicker lead-times make for happy customers.

INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

PVM Logistics enjoys advanced levels of inventory management, due to our storage capabilities. Partnering with us means that you get high-level controls over supplies that allow your business to gain quick access to supplement, order and quick distribution, from offshore inventories to real-time environments. The constant availability of stock leads to effective inventory management, and helps move products to the right place at the right time and at the right cost.
