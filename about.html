HISTORY

Manish Singhal, one-half of the maverick Singhal brothers, started out as a humble super-stockist back in 2002. He started his career learning the ropes of warehousing and supply-chain management with a variety of FMCG centered companies before venturing out on his own. It is in these formative years that Manish envisioned a 3PL company to ease the chaotic logistics market in India.

In 2008, four years after Vijay Singhal joined his brother, PVM Logistics was conceived officially. The experience and insight the brothers had gained, propelled them to create a supply chain company that had aspirations to work with the best by being the best. The goal was simple, give customers transparency, visibility and control over their own operations.

It was this passion that gave rise to a world class logistics company.

VISION

PVM Logistics, being a young and dynamic organization with a live-wire entrepreneurial spirit, aspires to disrupt the highly disoriented logistics sector in India

We aim to be pioneers in technological capabilities that shall ensure optimal results for any supply chain operation. PVM shall always strive to create a logistical environment based on International Logistics Standards.

Inclusivity, a customer-comes-first attitude, and interests of our stakeholders is what drives us to achieve excellence within and beyond our corporate doors.

The Geniuses Behind Our Work

Manish Singhal

(Founder & Managing Director)

Manish Singhal, Founder & Managing Director, completed his MBA from Indore before joining Eveready Batteries where he learned the fundamentals of warehousing and supply-chain management. In 2002 he started his own venture, and established himself as a super-stockist for some of India’s leading FMCG companies. Manish founded PVM Logistics in 2008, to fully establish himself as North India’s most trusted logistics partner that works across industries.

VIJAY SINGHAL

(Co-founder & Joint Managing Director)

Vijay Singhal, Co-founder & Joint Managing Director, joined his brother in 2004 and became an integral part of all future endeavors. It was their shared synergy that led to the inception of PVM Logistics. Today, Vijay spearheads some of the most innovative business strategies that help propel PVM to be the best.
