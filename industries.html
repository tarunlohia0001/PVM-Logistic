FMCG

PVM Logistics’ long-term expertise with FMCG logistics is backed by our thorough knowledge of FSSAI regulations. Our temperature controlled warehousing and distribution facilities adhere to all government regulations. We also have regular quality and integrity inspections to ensure that your FMCG goods stay fresh throughout transportation

HEALTHCARE & PHARMA

PVM Logistics understands the needs of the healthcare industry and their demand for a quick turnaround. We also understand that each second counts when it comes to healthcare, which is why we have developed highly-customizable logistical plans, to cater specifically to the needs of the healthcare industry.

INDUSTRIES RETAIL

PVM Logistics has a proven track record with the retail industry and this has allowed us to develop a robust logistics solution that can quickly be customized. We have also placed experts to assess stocks at feeder warehouses, and strategize a transport plan with zero-margin for error. Our warehousing takes away the added burden from your business, while also letting you enjoy the benefits of constant stock availability.

E-COMMERCE

PVM Logistics works with more than 1000 stockists across north India, and with our own fleet of 150 cold and dry vehicles, our domestic footprint is strong and ever-growing. We have the ability to deliver the required quality, speed and convenience demanded by today’s e-commerce corporations.

BEVERAGES

PVM Logistics’ long association with the beverage sector has pushed us to create tailor-made transport solutions for your product. Temperature controlled vehicles fitted with customizable racks, ensure that your product is fresh and undamaged at the time of delivery.

FOOD

PVM Logistics understands the demands of today’s restaurant industry, with both chefs and customers demanding the freshest of ingredients. Our warehouses ensure that products are constantly available, while our order management systems ensure that FSSAI regulations are met every step of the way, and the produce is delivered in our advanced temperature controlled vehicles.
